1. The short version

Card Swap does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. Your game progress stays on your phone.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. We recommend that you keep your phone's operating system up to date.
